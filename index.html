About Us

A Focused Place to Study and Grow

Concept Classes was started in Hanumangarh with a simple idea: students learn best when concepts are explained clearly and practised regularly. Our classrooms are kept small so that every student can ask questions freely.

Our faculty guides students from school-level topics to competitive exam preparation. Parents receive regular updates on attendance and test performance so they can support their child at home.

Our Programs

Courses We Offer

Structured programs for students at different stages of their preparation.

JEE Foundation

A concept-focused program that builds a strong base in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for future engineering entrance exams.

  • Duration 12 Months
  • Classes 4 Days / Week
  • Suitable for Class 9 to 11 students
  • Includes Concept Classes, Weekly Tests, Doubt Sessions

NEET Foundation

A steady, subject-wise program in Physics, Chemistry and Biology for students who aim to prepare early for medical entrance exams.

  • Duration 12 Months
  • Classes 5 Days / Week
  • Suitable for Class 9 to 11 students
  • Includes Subject Classes, Test Series, Doubt Support

Class 11–12 Science

Regular classroom coaching that supports school studies and prepares students for board exams and entrance tests together.

  • Duration 12 Months
  • Subjects Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics / Biology
  • Suitable for Class 11 and 12 Science students
  • Includes Regular Classes + Tests

Board Exam Preparation

A focused revision program that covers the full syllabus, with practice papers to build speed and confidence before the exams.

  • Duration 6 Months
  • Suitable for Class 10 and 12 students
  • Includes Revision Classes, Practice Papers, Doubt Sessions
